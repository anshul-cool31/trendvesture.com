Lustrous, resilient hair is not an accident of genetics; it is the predictable outcome of consistent, gentle haircare rituals. Just as modern skincare emphasizes the moisture barrier, contemporary hair science focuses on the health of the scalp microbiome and the preservation of the hair’s internal keratin bonds.

Incorporate these five essential habits into your weekly routine to repair damage and stimulate vibrant growth.

---

## 1. Treat the Scalp Like Facial Skin

Your scalp is an extension of your face, containing thousands of sebaceous glands and hair follicles. When product buildup, dead skin cells, and sweat accumulate, follicles become congested, impeding optimal growth.

- Use a gentle clarifying shampoo or salicylic acid scalp serum once every 10 to 14 days.
- Invert your head and massage your scalp with fingertips for four minutes daily to stimulate microcirculation.

---

## 2. Never Skip Thermal Heat Protectant

Heat styling tools operating above 180°C permanently degrade the protein structure of hair fibers, leading to split ends, brittleness, and dullness. Always apply a silicone or plant-oil heat shield spray from mid-lengths to ends before blow-drying, curling, or straightening.

---

## 3. Switch to Silk or Satin Pillowcases

Traditional cotton pillowcases create constant friction against hair cuticles as you toss and turn at night, causing frizz, moisture depletion, and mechanical breakage. Sleeping on pure mulberry silk allows strands to glide smoothly without snagging.
