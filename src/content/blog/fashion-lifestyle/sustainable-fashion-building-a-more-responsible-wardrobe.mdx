The modern fashion industry’s environmental toll is well documented: millions of tons of synthetic textiles discarded into landfills annually, microplastics polluting waterways, and staggering carbon emissions from fast-fashion supply chains.

However, embracing sustainable fashion does not mean abandoning personal style or dressing exclusively in unbleached linen sacks. It is a philosophy of mindful custodianship—investing in well-made garments and extending their lifecycle through care.

---

## 1. The 30-Wear Standard

Before purchasing any new garment, ask yourself one simple question: "Will I commit to wearing this piece at least thirty times?" If the honest answer is no, leave it on the rack. This simple mental filter eliminates impulsive, trend-chasing purchases.

---

## 2. Garment Longevity and Care

The most sustainable garment is the one already hanging in your closet. Laundering clothes less frequently, washing in cold water, line drying, and learning basic mending (sewing on a loose button, debobbling wool knits with a sweater comb) extends garment lifespan by years.
