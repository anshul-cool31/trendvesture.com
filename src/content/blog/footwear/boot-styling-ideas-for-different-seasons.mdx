Boots are the foundational anchor of transitional and cold-weather dressing. A pair of well-maintained boots lends weight, structure, and rugged elegance to an outfit that lighter footwear simply cannot replicate.

Here is how to master boot styling across the changing seasons.

---

## 1. The Knee-High Leather Riding Boot

A sleek knee-high boot in smooth black or burgundy box-calf leather is the ideal companion to midi skirts and knitted dresses. By allowing the skirt hem to overlap the top of the boot shaft, you create an unbroken vertical column of color that keeps you warm and look impeccably tailored.

---

## 2. The Classic Chelsea Boot

With its elasticated side gussets and pull-tabs, the Chelsea boot is effortless to slip on and off. Style suede Chelsea boots with slim-straight denim and a cashmere sweater for an iconic off-duty look.
