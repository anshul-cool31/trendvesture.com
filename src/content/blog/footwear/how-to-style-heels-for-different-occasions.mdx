A well-designed heel changes the geometry of the body: lengthening the leg line, lifting posture, and bestowing instant poise. However, styling heels today is less about torturous skyscraper heights and more about sophisticated proportion control and sustainable comfort.

Below are guidelines for choosing and styling the perfect heel for every scenario.

---

## 1. The Modern Kitten Heel (35–50mm)

Kitten heels have staged a triumphant, permanent return. Their low, sculpted pitch offers all-day walkability while injecting vintage French-girl charm into cropped jeans, slip dresses, and midi skirts.

---

## 2. The Classic 85mm Pointed-Toe Pump

When full formal glamour is required, the 85mm pointed-toe pump remains the gold standard. It creates an elongated arch without the unbearable foot strain of 100mm+ stilettos. Pair with floor-grazing tailored trousers so that only the sharp pointed toe peeks through as you stride.
