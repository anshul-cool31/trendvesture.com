Online fashion shopping provides unprecedented access to global designers, archival vintage treasures, and niche labels. However, it also comes with familiar perils: opening a package only to find the color looks nothing like the screen, or the medium trousers fit like an extra-small.

Becoming a master online shopper requires treating it like an exact science rather than a game of luck.

---

## 1. Measure Your Garments, Not Your Body

Body measurements can be misleading because different fabrics require different degrees of 'ease' to move and drape properly.
- Take your best-fitting pair of trousers and spread them flat on a table.
- Measure the exact waist width, front rise, thigh width, and inseam.
- Save these precise numbers in your smartphone notes. When shopping online, compare the retailer's garment measurement chart against your best-fitting piece.

---

## 2. Watch the Runway Video

Never rely solely on static, heavily retouched studio photographs where models are pinned in the back. Always look for the retailer’s runway or catwalk video. Video reveals the honest truth about a garment: how the fabric moves, whether it creases when walking, and where the hem truly falls.
