You do not need to spend thousands of dollars to achieve a sophisticated, editorial fashion aesthetic. Today's high street and vintage marketplaces offer exceptional pieces if you possess the trained eye to spot quality materials and superior tailoring details.

Here is your insider guide to hunting for high-value fashion finds.

---

## 1. Inspect Fabric Labels First

Before you fall in love with a garment’s color or cut on the rack, examine the inner fabric composition label:
- Avoid 100% acrylic or polyester knits, which pill after three washes and trap odor.
- Seek out 100% cotton, 100% linen, or wool blends containing at least 70% natural animal fibers. High-street brands frequently produce limited runs of 100% natural fiber garments at very reasonable price points.

---

## 2. Invest in Tailoring

A $50 pair of high-street trousers taken to a skilled local tailor for $20 (hemming the leg length to the perfect break, nipping in the waistband) will look infinitely better than a $400 designer pair that doesn't fit your unique proportions. Tailoring is the ultimate budget secret weapon.
