The concept of the capsule wardrobe—originally coined in the 1970s by boutique owner Susie Faux and popularized by Donna Karan—remains the gold standard for intentional living. A practical capsule wardrobe is not an ascetic exercise in deprivation; it is a curated collection of interchangeable garments that maximize outfit versatility.

Below is your blueprint for creating a 30-piece seasonal capsule that unlocks over 100 unique combinations.

---

## The 30-Piece Framework
- **Tops (10):** 3 crewneck tees, 2 Oxford button-downs, 1 silk blouse, 2 fine-gauge knits, 1 chunky cardigan, 1 sweatshirt.
- **Bottoms (6):** 2 pairs of straight-leg denim (one raw indigo, one ecru/black), 2 tailored trousers, 1 casual chino, 1 midi skirt.
- **Dresses / One-Pieces (2):** 1 tailored shirt dress, 1 versatile slip dress.
- **Outerwear (4):** 1 tailored blazer, 1 classic trench coat, 1 leather jacket, 1 heavy wool coat.
- **Shoes (5):** 1 white leather sneaker, 1 loafer, 1 ankle boot, 1 dress shoe / heel, 1 seasonal sandal or winter boot.
- **Bags (3):** 1 structured tote, 1 crossbody bag, 1 evening clutch.
